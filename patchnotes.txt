v8.14.0 — UPDATE — 2026-07-15 12:06 SAST
  Added (HD Remaster only — Leader Mode):
    - Archer support: no longer a dead slot — each support archer grants
      the leader ×1.4 TOTAL attack speed, applied AFTER all additive
      augments (two archers = ×1.96; card shows "+96% total spd").
  Fixed (the quirks that undermined the mode):
    - Support on-hit riders (burn, slow, splash, priest heal) now work
      for EVERY leader, not just archer/ranger/assassin: mage, cannon,
      ice and fire leaders apply them to their primary target, and the
      knight leader applies them to every enemy its zones strike (priest
      heal fires once per swing, not once per enemy, to keep it sane).
    - Ranger support ("hunts fast/flying") now steers every
      nearest-to-base leader (archer, mage, cannon, ice, fire) — it
      previously only affected an archer leader. Assassin keeps its
      strongest-enemy identity.
    - Elemental conversion (ice/fire) now applies to ALL leader types,
      with shots/booms/damage numbers in the element's colour.
    - Cards updated to match: archer support shows "→ +40% ldr spd";
      the "hunts fast" line shows on every leader it actually affects.

v8.13.0 — UPDATE — 2026-07-15 11:58 SAST
  Changed (HD Remaster only — Leader Mode support rework):
    - Ice Wizard support: slow reduced 30% → 20%, but it now CONVERTS the
      leader's hits to ICE damage (so ice-typed run buffs/curses like
      Permafrost or Frozen Heart apply to the leader).
    - Fire Wizard support: still applies its burn, and now also converts
      the leader's hits to FIRE damage. If both elemental supports are
      equipped, the later slot decides the element — order matters.
    - Knight support: slow increased 20% → 30%.
    - Leader projectiles/damage numbers take the converted element's
      colour. Support-knight AoE zones are no longer drawn in Leader Mode
      (the field knight only acts there when it IS the leader).
  Added:
    - Leader Mode support readout on the unit stat cards: the leader's
      card lists everything it receives (support dmg multiplier, element
      conversion, splash, burn, combined slow, heal-per-hit, targeting)
      and each support's card shows what it grants ("→ +60% ldr dmg",
      "→ ice dmg · +20% slow", … archer honestly shows "no ldr effect").

v8.12.0 — UPDATE — 2026-07-14 13:36 SAST
  Added (HD Remaster only):
    - Per-unit augment stat cards: the defender formation is spread wider

v8.11.0 — UPDATE — 2026-07-14 09:41 SAST
  Added (HD Remaster only):
    - TIME-TO-CASTLE movement: every enemy now carries a `ttc` — the
      number of SECONDS it takes to march from spawn to the wall —
      instead of a pixel speed. It is derived from the same data-table
      speeds and tuning as before, so desktop pacing is mathematically
      identical (verified to floating-point precision). Wave scaling
      divides the time as before; speed AUGMENTS (run buffs/challenges,
      e.g. Cursed Treasury) now adjust the time directly: +25% speed
      means exactly 25% less time to reach the castle. Slows still cut
      the movement rate while active.
    - Phone-scaling arena: on portrait screens the logical battlefield
      switches from 1600×900 to 900×1600 so the game fills a phone
      instead of a letterboxed sliver. Because enemies move on
      time-to-castle, the difficulty is IDENTICAL on any arena size
      (same 18.7s wave-1 march on desktop and phone). The arena re-fits
      on rotation/resize but never mid-combat — a run keeps its field
      until it ends.
    - Phone/touch UI: safe-area (notch) support, no tap-zoom on
      controls, compact HUD, full-width menu buttons, damage meter
      pinned to the top of the field (it used to sit on the castle) and
      starting collapsed on small screens, moon/badge/footer layout
      fixed so nothing overlaps the title or edition chips.

v8.10.1 — HOTFIX — 2026-07-14 02:52 SAST
  Fixed:
    - HD Remaster load time: the generated HD sprites were unoptimised
      PNGs (9.7 MB total — bigger than the whole classic game), so on a
      hosted connection the art was often still downloading when Play was
      pressed. The generator now encodes WebP (visually lossless q0.9,
      alpha preserved; background q0.85), shrinking the entire HD set to
      1.9 MB — actually lighter than the classic sprites (5.5 MB). Whole
      HD page + all assets now settle in well under a second locally and
      load ~5× faster over the network. Fallback behaviour is unchanged:
      any missing/unsupported file still drops back to the classic PNG.

v8.0.0 — MAJOR — 2026-07-02 14:04 SAST
  Added — eight new Run Challenge modes (behavioural, not just modifiers):
    - Command Mode: units don't auto-attack; click to command ALL units to
      strike the aim point. The Knight holds a persistent AoE at the last
      click and relocates on the next click.
    - Trapmaster: click to place traps (one per equipped unit type, cycling);
      traps deal damage/slow/burn/heal and expire or trigger once.
    - Spellbook Mode: each equipped unit becomes an active ability with its
      own cooldown, cast from a button bar under the battlefield.
    - Leader Mode: only slot 1 attacks; the other slots add on-hit support
      mods (burn/slow/splash/lifesteal/damage/targeting). Loadout order matters.
    - Bounty Hunt: each wave marks a 👑 enemy — kill it for a wave bonus
      (coins/heal/damage/attack-speed) before it reaches the base.
    - Overwatch: units charge a powerful shot (charge bars); click to release
      every charged unit as a strong volley.
    - Chain Reaction: enemies explode on death, hurting nearby foes; direct
      damage is 20% lower to reward clustering.
    - No Wall: no base HP — limited lives; each enemy that reaches the base
      costs one, and healing slowly restores lives.
