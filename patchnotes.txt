v9.2.0 — UPDATE — 2026-07-14 02:15 SAST
  Added:
    - Pre-baked HD sprite set (sprites/hd/, 30 PNGs) generated by the new
      tools/make-hd-sprites.js: vibrance/contrast, directional light and
      a baked silhouette shadow on every unit and enemy sprite.
    - Enemy walk animation: a second <type>_walk.png step pose alternated
      every 34px of ground covered — fast enemies scurry, slowed enemies
      trudge, flyers flap.
    - Unit shooting animation: a <id>_shoot.png recoil/cast pose shown

      (auto-attacks, Command/Overwatch strikes, Spellbook casts).

    - All HD sprite files are optional: a missing idle falls back to the
      classic PNG via the runtime remaster; a missing pose frame simply
      disables that sprite's animation. Synced from the served branch's
      game-hd.html (v8.9.0).

v8.0.0 — MAJOR — 2026-07-02 14:04 SAST
  Added — eight new Run Challenge modes (behavioural, not just modifiers):
    - Command Mode: units don't auto-attack; click to command ALL units to
      strike the aim point. The Knight holds a persistent AoE at the last
      click and relocates on the next click.
    - Trapmaster: click to place traps (one per equipped unit type, cycling);
      traps deal damage/slow/burn/heal and expire or trigger once.
    - Spellbook Mode: each equipped unit becomes an active ability with its
      own cooldown, cast from a button bar under the battlefield.
    - Leader Mode: only slot 1 attacks; the other slots add on-hit support
      mods (burn/slow/splash/lifesteal/damage/targeting). Loadout order matters.
    - Bounty Hunt: each wave marks a 👑 enemy — kill it for a wave bonus
      (coins/heal/damage/attack-speed) before it reaches the base.
    - Overwatch: units charge a powerful shot (charge bars); click to release
      every charged unit as a strong volley.
    - Chain Reaction: enemies explode on death, hurting nearby foes; direct
      damage is 20% lower to reward clustering.
    - No Wall: no base HP — limited lives; each enemy that reaches the base
      costs one, and healing slowly restores lives.
