Goal: survive as many waves as possible. Every 5th wave is a boss; beating it lets you pick a temporary run buff.

Clicking: click an enemy on the battlefield to deal click damage to it.

Units: buy them in the Shop, equip up to 5 in the Units tab. Each type plays differently.

Death: when base HP hits 0 the wave resets to 0, but you keep coins, units, permanent upgrades and talents. Run buffs reset.

Talents: spend coins to buy talent points, then spend points on permanent upgrades.

Progress auto-saves to your browser.
