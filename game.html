📖 How to Play

Goal

Defend your keep against waves of enemies. Survive as long as you can — each wave is tougher than the last.

Clicking & Shooting

During combat, click anywhere on the battlefield to fire a shot toward that spot. A shot fires even if you click empty space. Talents can add critical hits, splash damage, and an AOE shockwave that blasts the area around your click.

Units

Buy units in the Upgrades screen and equip up to 5 at once. Each plays differently:

🏹 Archer — cheap, fast single-target.
🔮 Mage — slower, splash damage.
🛡️ Knight — guards the base, slows & damages nearby foes.
💣 Cannon — slow but huge area damage.
✨ Priest — heals the base over time.
🎯 Ranger — prioritises fast/flying enemies.
🗡️ Assassin — massive crits vs tanks & bosses.
❄️ Ice Wizard — slows enemies.
🔥 Fire Wizard — burn damage over time.
🔧 Engineer — buffs your units' damage & repairs the base.

Enemies & Bosses

Enemies range from grunts to fast runners, tanky brutes and flyers. They scale in health, speed and reward over time, and later waves bring stronger types. Every 5th wave is a boss.

Boss Rewards (temporary)

When a boss dies the game pauses and offers 3 temporary buffs. Pick one to continue. These last only for the current run and reset when you die.

Some choices are ☠ Cursed — they grant a powerful boon but come with a real downside (for example Pyromancer's Pact: 3× fire damage but half physical damage). High risk, high reward.

Permanent Upgrades & Talents

Spend coins to buy talent points, then unlock permanent upgrades across four branches: Click, Units (general), Specific units, and Defence. These are kept forever.

Run Challenges

From the menu, open Run Challenges to pick one optional modifier before a run. Challenges make the run harder but more rewarding (e.g. Swarm Mode: faster spawns, +50% coins). Only one is active per run, it shows on the combat bar, and it resets on death.

Death

If your base HP reaches 0 the run ends and the wave resets to 0. You keep coins, owned units, unit levels, equipped loadout, permanent upgrades and talents. Only temporary boss buffs and the active challenge reset.

Saving & Backups

Progress auto-saves to your browser. In the Upgrades → Save Manager panel you can Export your save as a text file and Import it later or on another device. Imports are validated, so a bad file won't overwrite your progress.

Modding: advanced users can edit run-buffs.js next to the game to change the boss reward pool.

⚙ Settings

Rendering options only — gameplay is unchanged.

Battlefield Resolution

Changes the internal canvas render resolution. Higher values make sprites sharper but can use more GPU power. The visible battlefield size stays around 1600×900 by default and scales down when needed to fit the screen.
